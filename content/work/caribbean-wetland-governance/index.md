Through **Hub-Terra**, I held a lead technical role in two international consultancy assignments related to wetland governance and planning across **Antigua and Barbuda, Belize, Cuba, the Dominican Republic, Grenada, Jamaica, Saint Lucia and Suriname**.

The work included comparative institutional assessment, review and development of planning instruments, governance and capacity analysis, indicators and monitoring frameworks, implementation roadmaps, and consultation and validation processes with national counterparts.

The assignments connected environmental evidence with institutional and policy analysis to support practical implementation of integrated coastal and wetland-management priorities.
