My doctoral research at the **University of Córdoba** focuses on biological invasions, spatial dynamics and management-oriented decision support in Mediterranean ecosystems.

Current work includes the analysis of plant-invasion processes along environmental and transport gradients and the use of spatially explicit modelling to explore long-term management options. This research builds on field observations, geospatial data and stakeholder-informed approaches developed across my doctoral work.
