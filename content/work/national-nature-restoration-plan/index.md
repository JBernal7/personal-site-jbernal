At **Tragsatec**, I was responsible for the methodological development and technical execution of the spatial-modelling component linked to nature-restoration planning.

I developed reproducible workflows for data preparation and predictor generation, zonal and azonal modelling approaches, validation, spatial-bias control, ecological and cartographic consistency checks, scripts, documentation and spatial outputs.

The work supported evidence-based planning for terrestrial habitats and species within the broader national restoration framework.
