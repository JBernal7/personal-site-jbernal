For **Hub-Terra**, I was lead author of the ecosystem-services assessment developed for **LIFE LYNXCONNECT** for the Regional Government of Andalusia.

The work applied **MAES/CICES** framing to organise ecosystem-service evidence, indicators and management implications, and incorporated recommendations for post-LIFE monitoring and evaluation. The assignment connected ecological evidence with a structured assessment framework designed to support conservation planning and project evaluation.
