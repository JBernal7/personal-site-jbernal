For **Hub-Terra**, I led the technical development and principal execution of a municipal strategy for the detection, prioritisation and management of invasive alien flora in **Colmenar (Málaga)**.

The work integrated field surveys, geospatial analysis and spatial modelling with operational prioritisation, species-specific management guidance and stakeholder engagement. I also facilitated the final technical workshop, translating the evidence base into practical municipal management priorities.
