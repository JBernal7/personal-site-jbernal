I facilitated the final technical workshop of Hub-Terra's municipal invasive-flora project in **Colmenar (Málaga)**.

The session connected species identification, management priorities, spatial evidence and stakeholder perspectives, helping translate the project's technical outputs into practical local decision-making and awareness.
